Rule syntax reference
Rules are comparisons of indicators, joined with AND / OR. Comparators: < > <= >=. All case-insensitive.

CLOSE · SMA(n) / EMA(n) · RSI(n) · MOM(n)

Examples (click to fill the form):
  • Dip buyer — buy RSI(14) < 30 AND CLOSE > SMA(200), sell RSI(14) > 70
  • Trend follower — buy SMA(20) > SMA(50), sell SMA(20) < SMA(50)
  • Buy & hold — buy CLOSE > 0, sell CLOSE < 0
