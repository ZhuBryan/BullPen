Simulated market : price is produced, not downloaded

Trading bots post real orders into the matching engine and trade against each other. Nobody supplies a price: the candles below are what they actually traded at. Market makers quote both sides and skew to shed inventory, momentum bots chase moves, mean-reverters fade them, noise traders add uninformed flow.

P&L is zero-sum: every dollar won is a dollar lost by another agent.

Execution and transaction cost analysis

A parent order worked through the live book. Child orders match against whatever the agents are quoting, so the algorithm pays the spread and moves the book rather than filling at an assumed price. Cost is implementation shortfall against the arrival price, split into what the traded shares cost and the opportunity cost of the shares that never filled. Positive always means worse for the trader.

Research agent · searches, then discounts itself

The agent proposes strategies, backtests them, and refines. The interesting part is the critic: search enough variations and one will look brilliant by pure luck, so every result is measured against what luck alone would produce after that many trials. A verdict of not supported is the normal, honest outcome.
